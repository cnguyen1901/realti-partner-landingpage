What we collect

When you join the waitlist, we collect your email address and, if you choose to share them, your first name and your answers to our short questions (where you are in your journey, what describes you, your city or state, and whether you're open to a chat with our founders).

We also record how you found us, such as the website that sent you or the campaign link you clicked, and an approximate location (country, region and city) based on your connection. We don't store your IP address.

We measure visits with Vercel Web Analytics, which doesn't use cookies and doesn't identify you personally.

How we use it

We use your information to tell you about RealtiPartner, invite you to early access, and understand which ways of finding us work best. If you said you're open to a chat, we may email you to schedule one.

Who we share it with

Your choices
